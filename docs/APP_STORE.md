## Title
Foundry Stems — Audio Stem Organizer

## Subtitle
Billet Your Stems. Export the Manifest.

## Description

100% local. 100% offline. 100% yours.

A session folder full of stems is raw material without structure. Foundry Stems forges order from chaos — tagging each billet by role, tracking heat, and exporting a bloom manifest your collaborators can immediately use.

Drop your stems in. Tag them by role. Export a clean manifest for your collaborators.

**STEM ORGANIZATION**
• Import audio stems by drag-and-drop
• Assign each stem a role: Pulse (drums), Lead (melody), Atmosphere (pads/ambient), Spark (effects)
• Tag stem type: Brass Weave, Iron Grain, Steel Spine, Copper Mist
• Add quench notes: mixing notes, processing hints, stem history

**SESSION MANAGEMENT**
• Named hearth sessions per project
• Heat intensity per stem (0–100) for relative importance tracking
• Autosave — never lose your session organization
• Load and save .forgeledger session files

**BLOOM MANIFEST**
• Export a structured .bloommanifest JSON file
• Lists all stems, roles, types, heat values, and notes
• Share manifests with collaborators and mixing engineers
• Import manifests to restore session organization

**FOR**
• Music producers organizing large session folders
• Mixing engineers receiving stems from clients
• Sound designers organizing libraries
• Producers collaborating with remote engineers
